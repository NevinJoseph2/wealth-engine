How to Install as a Mobile App

🍏 On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Open this app page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square icon with an upward arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top right. It installs directly with its own app icon and runs full-screen without Safari browser bars!

🤖 On Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge):

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm installation. The app will launch as a standalone application.
